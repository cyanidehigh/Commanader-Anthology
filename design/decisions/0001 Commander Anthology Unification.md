# 0001 Commander Anthology Unification
#type/decision #status/accepted #project/commander-anthology

Links: [[../DDS]], [[../areas/Shared Core]]

## Decision

CCBuilder and Commander Sim are one product direction: Commander Anthology.

They are two surfaces over one shared platform, not separate long-term products.

## Consequences

- There is one root [[../../README|README]].
- There is one root [[../../HANDOFF|HANDOFF]].
- The design system lives in `design/`.
- Shared truth should move into a common core instead of staying duplicated
  across legacy projects.
