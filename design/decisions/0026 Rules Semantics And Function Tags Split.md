# 0026 Rules Semantics And Function Tags Split
#type/decision #status/accepted #area/ai-architecture #area/card-function-grammar

Links: [[../DDS]], [[../areas/Card Function Grammar]], [[../areas/Card Codex]], [[../areas/AI Architecture]], [[../areas/Player Style]], [[0020 AI Decision Contract]], [[0015 Player Style Data Boundary]], [[0016 Legal Move Package Boundary]]

## Decision

Anthology separates exact card rules semantics from strategic function tags.

The AI and rules engine need exact rules semantics to understand what a card
can legally do. The function-tagging layer exists to help Anthology explain
cards, deck construction, AI scoring, and player style in familiar player
language.

## Exact Rules Semantics

Exact rules semantics are authoritative gameplay data.

They belong to the Card Codex, rules engine, legal move package, validation
layer, and execution layer.

They answer questions such as:

- What legal actions does this card allow?
- What timing permissions apply?
- What costs must be paid?
- What targets are legal?
- What choices are explicit?
- What zones and objects are affected?
- What state changes happen after validation?

The AI may consume the resulting legal move package, but it does not own this
truth.

## Function Tags

Function tags are descriptive strategic labels.

They answer questions such as:

- What role does this card normally perform?
- What kind of deck package does it support?
- What kind of play pattern does it represent?
- How should Anthology explain this card to a player?
- How does this relate to the user's observed play style?

Example tag grammar:

```text
Tutor > Creature
Tutor > To Hand
Mana > Sink
Steal > Creature
Removal > Creature
Graveyard > Recursion
```

Function tags are useful for deck building, recommendation explanation, player
style analysis, and AI move explanations.

They are not legal authority.

## AI Boundary

The AI opponent must only choose from legal moves generated by the engine.

Function tags may help score or explain an already-legal move, but they must
not:

- create a move
- bypass legal move generation
- bypass final execution validation
- override card rules
- expose hidden information
- consume human player-style data

## Player Style Boundary

Player style may be summarized through function tags so the Builder and future
LLM explanation layer can talk naturally to the player.

This does not make player style available to the AI opponent.

## Consequences

- Rules work remains precise and testable.
- Builder and coaching features can become helpful before the full rules engine
  understands every card.
- AI explanation can use familiar player concepts without corrupting legality.
- Player style can be expressed in human terms without becoming AI training
  input.
