# CCBuilder
#type/product-surface #status/active #area/ccbuilder

Links: [[DDS]], [[Commander Anthology]], [[areas/Shared Core]], [[areas/Player Style]]

CCBuilder is the deck-building, collection, search, import, legality, and
recommendation surface.

On desktop, CCBuilder can use the full Anthology toolset.

On Android, deck building is intentionally lighter and companion-oriented. The
mobile app is not the full Anthology program.
